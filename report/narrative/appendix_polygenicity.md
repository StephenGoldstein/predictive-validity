### How polygenicity was measured and tested

**The unit.** Diseases with a US-developed Phase 2 entry in 2008–2019, outside oncology and infectious disease, on programme build {{polygenicity.build_id}}: {{polygenicity.cohort.diseases|int}} diseases with {{polygenicity.cohort.entries|int}} Phase 2 and 3 entries ({{polygenicity.cohort.phase2|int}} Phase 2), of which {{polygenicity.cohort.with_target|int}} have a resolved primary target. The tertiles, their membership and every definition below were fixed in a dated comment on issue #147 ({{polygenicity.comment_id}}) before any outcome was joined.

**The score.** For each disease, the GWAS Catalog (release 2026-11-19) study of the disease, or failing that of a subtype, with public harmonised summary statistics, at least 90% European ancestry, a case-control design and a genome-wide platform (over 200,000 SNPs), taking the largest by effective sample size (4 / (1/cases + 1/controls)). LDSC (the Python 3 port, 2.0.1) with the European LD scores on the HapMap3 SNPs gave the SNP heritability on the observed scale, converted to the liability scale with the study's case fraction and the disease's population prevalence: the GBD 2021 global prevalence of the matching cause (all ages, both sexes, the GBD 2023 round, mapped by hand as the WHO causes are), or where GBD has no cause, the Pan-UK Biobank European case fraction of the matching ICD-10 code. Independent loci are the lead SNPs after PLINK clumping of the same summary statistics at p < 5 × 10⁻⁸, r² < 0.1 within 1 Mb against the European 1000 Genomes panel cut to HapMap3 SNPs, with the extended MHC counted once. The score is loci per unit of liability-scale heritability; a disease scores only when the heritability estimate has z ≥ 4 and the liability-scale value lies in (0, 1]. Tertile cutpoints over the scored diseases: {{polygenicity.cutpoints.score.lower|x2}} and {{polygenicity.cutpoints.score.upper|x2}}. **The registered tests.** P1: among Phase 2 and 3 entries with a resolved primary target, a stop in the "target not causal" failure mode, top tertile against bottom, from a modified Poisson model adjusted as the target-origin test is (disease area, modality, entry period, novelty and phase), with errors clustered by disease. P2: among Phase 2 entries, advance to Phase 3 or approval, adjusted for area, modality, entry period, novelty and precedent. P3: on the same entries, the interaction of the top tertile with rare-variant or Mendelian genetic support (the highest germline tier) on advance, from one model holding both and their product, with the genetics ratio within each tertile reported beside it when the test is estimable; it is estimable only when each of the four cells holds at least 15 target sets and one event. Each p is one-sided in the registered direction (more not-causal stops, fewer advances, a smaller genetics benefit) and Holm's correction runs across the three. The middle tertile is reported, in no test.

**The sensitivities, as amended on 2026-11-24.** GWAS size adds the study's log10 effective sample size (4 / (1/cases + 1/controls)) to the covariates and repeats P1–P3 with Holm within itself. Mendelian form splits all {{polygenicity.cohort.diseases|int}} unit diseases, the no-score group included, by whether a gene carries Orphanet, ClinGen, Genomics England or Gene2Phenotype evidence for the disease, a subtype or a disease at Lin ≥ 0.8 in the full Open Targets association file, with "no Mendelian form" in place of the top tertile, again P1–P3 with Holm within. The negative control is P1's model with the outcome a stop whose traced cause is a sponsor decision or operational, entries with a safety stop left out, reported two-sided outside any family; no association is expected, and one would point to confounding by disease area or sponsor interest. The continuous-score and concentration sensitivities registered on 2026-11-23 were dropped in that decision.

**Descriptive checks, none registered.** Admitting heritability estimates at z ≥ 2 ({{polygenicity.descriptives.z_at_least_2.diseases|int}} diseases); diseases whose prevalence comes from GBD alone ({{polygenicity.descriptives.gbd_k_only.diseases|int}}); the Catalog's genome-wide significant associations across all studies of the disease, merged within 500 kb, in place of the clumped loci ({{polygenicity.descriptives.catalog_loci.diseases|int}}); and the count of GWAS-linked genes per 10,000 cases from the Open Targets evidence as the exposure ({{polygenicity.descriptives.gwas_genes.diseases|int}}). Where O'Connor and colleagues (2019) published a model-based effective number of causal variants for a scored disease ({{polygenicity.descriptives.oconnor.n|int}} diseases), its Spearman correlation with the score is {{polygenicity.descriptives.oconnor.spearman|x2}}.

**Burden.** Of 2021 global DALYs (WHO Global Health Estimates), {{polygenicity.daly.shares.all.mendelian_form|pct0}} fall in causes mapped to a profiled disease with a Mendelian form, {{polygenicity.daly.shares.all.drugged_large_effect|pct0}} in causes mapped to a disease where a gene with rare-variant or Mendelian evidence is the primary target of a programme that reached Phase 2 or 3 or was approved, and {{polygenicity.daly.shares.all.either|pct0}} in either; of non-communicable DALYs, {{polygenicity.daly.shares.ncd.mendelian_form|pct0}}, {{polygenicity.daly.shares.ncd.drugged_large_effect|pct0}} and {{polygenicity.daly.shares.ncd.either|pct0}}. The flags cover {{polygenicity.daly.flags.diseases|int}} profiled diseases: {{polygenicity.daly.flags.mendelian_form|int}} with a Mendelian form and {{polygenicity.daly.flags.drugged_large_effect|int}} with such a drugged gene; WHO causes are mapped to MONDO by hand (`pipeline/data/who_ghe_cause_mondo.csv`), and a cause counts when any disease it maps to carries the flag.
